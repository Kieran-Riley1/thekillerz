About Us

The Killerz are one of the original tribute acts to The Killers, and winners of a National Tribute Music Award. We play theatres, tribute festivals, weddings and corporate shows across the UK and further afield.

We have shared stages with Chris Evans’ Car Fest, Butlins, Warner Hotels, Foodies Festivals and Alexandra Palace.

The full show travels with its own visual backdrops, lighting and a blast of Killer confetti, so a venue near you gets the Las Vegas superstars without the flight.

Proud Ambassadors

Raising awareness for the OurJay Foundation — installing publicly accessible defibrillators in memory of Jamie Rees.

Meet the Band

MIKE

Vocals

Mike started off singing musical theatre and contemporary music, going on to pass all of his grades with a distinction. He was part of various bands and solo projects before becoming the deputy to the lead singer for The Killerz. He went on to take over the role fully and has been the lead vocalist of the band for over 10 years.

GRANT

Drums

Grant first started learning to play the drums at the age of 7, showing a natural talent for it. He went on to study Pop Music & Music Technology at Wolverhampton University, passing his grade 8 exam. Grant's drumming and musical technical skills are an integral part of the band.

LEWIS

Guitar

Music runs through Lewis' blood. He learnt saxophone whilst at junior school and keyboard throughout high school, reaching grade 4. He started learning guitar at 17, even taught for a while by ex Killerz guitarist Paul Humphreys before forming rock band Speaking in Shadows with Sam.

SAM

Bass

Sam started playing bass at the age of 13. He studied music at school but has no formal training, instead teaching himself everything he knows. From 2010 – 2018 he played bass in successful unsigned band Speaking in Shadows with bandmate Lewis.
